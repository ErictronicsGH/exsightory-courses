Card 1 · When, and which level


PlotThe PLAN's plot: one slow waveform (grey), its sampled-and-held version (teal, steps in time), and that held value quantized (amber, steps in amplitude). Lower f_s and the steps widen in time. Lower N and they coarsen in amplitude: two independent kinds of coarseness, one on each axis. A Rounding | Truncating switch (accepted), because the course's converter truncates (F2).





Quantizer


The waveform is 1.65 + 1.2·sin(2π·0.5 Hz·t) V on a 0–3.3 V converter: slow on purpose (the PLAN's "one slow waveform"). Low N values are used so the levels can be seen; at 12 bits one level is 0.806 mV, far finer than the line.





Card 2 · Resolution: how many levels


EquationLSB = range / 2N, with N as a slider and the PLAN's two worked examples. The numeric fires first (8 bits on 0–10 V).






Worked examples: 8 bits on 10 V → 39.06 mV (a datasheet that divides by 255 gives 39.22 mV); 12 bits on 3.3 V → 0.806 mV. Every extra bit halves the step.





Card 3 · Quantization error is a floor (the centrepiece)


PlotTop: the staircase, input against the level it is given. Bottom: the error, the level minus the input (so truncating reads 0 to −1 LSB), with ±½ LSB lines. The marker sits at 8.540 V. Zoom near 8.5 V shows code 219's band, 8.535 to 8.574 V. N runs 4 to 12, and the sawtooth's height shrinks with every bit but never reaches zero. The Rounding | Truncating switch (accepted) shows truncation's band, 0 to −1 LSB: the same width, shifted.

Card 4 · More bits are not more accuracy


flash, no benchThe PLAN gives this card only the flash quiz (a 16-bit ADC on a ±1 % reference: is it accurate to one part in 65,536? No, to about 1 %). Its steps are 0.0015 % each; the reference moves all of them together by up to 1 %, about 655 steps. Proposal: no bench. The seed S34 is answered by card 8.





Card 5 · SAR: a binary search


AnimationDraw stand-inThe PLAN's search at 4 bits on 0–3.3 V for an input of 2.17 V: each step draws a trial level on the bar, keeps or drops the bit, and halves the window. The spec already exists from the template's dry run and is revised in this unit (see D3 below).








Card 6 · Sigma-delta: fast and coarse, then filtered


PlotA first-order modulator: the input in, a 1-bit stream out (ticks), and running averages over the last 16, 64 and 256 bits converging on the input. The density of 1s is the value; the resolution comes from how many bits you average. The PLAN names a Coding Bench, which is not built. Its own fallback is a Plot with a precomputed series, and that is what this shows (D2).

Card 8 · ENOB: the honest number of bits


EquationENOB = (SINAD − 1.76) / 6.02, graphed against SINAD from 40 to 100 dB, with the PLAN's two examples marked: the ideal 12-bit converter, 74.0 dB → 12 bits, and the datasheet's 62 dB → 10.0 bits. Every 6.02 dB is one bit.
