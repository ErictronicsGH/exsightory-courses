Card 3 · Level shifting: 5 V logic into a 3.3 V pin


Electronics widget deferred → demonstration (D1)poeThe PLAN's fixture level-shift: a 5 V output behind its own 47 Ω, into a 3.3 V input whose clamp diode (a 1N4148 model) goes to the 3.3 V rail. Try the direct wire, then the 10 k / 20 k divider, then the FAULT (the resistors swapped). Values are from the diode's SPICE model, with the 10 MΩ meter on the pin.







F1: wired straight, the meter does not read “about 3.3 V” as the PLAN's Look for says. It reads 4.03 V, a diode-drop above the rail, and 20.6 mA flows into the 3.3 V rail, which must have a load to absorb it. F5: the swapped divider gives 1.66 V, not 1.67, once the 47 Ω is counted. The input thresholds drawn are 0.25 and 0.75 of VDD (0.825 and 2.475 V), those of the 3.3 V MCU the card names.

Card 5 · What does a floating input read?


Electronics widget deferred → demonstration (D1)poeA logic input with nothing driving it. On the left is the input's band: low below 0.825 V, high above 2.475 V, undefined between. Floating, the pin's level wanders, and here it is drawn wandering across the whole range, rails included (F2). Then come the meter (the meter becomes the pull-down), the pull-up and the pull-down.







F2: the Flow says the floating pin “sits somewhere between a guaranteed low and a guaranteed high”. The card's own FAULT (a button that “reads HIGH on the bench every time”) needs it to sit near a rail. With the meter on: an input leaking ≤ 50 nA into 10 MΩ gives ≤ 0.5 V, and typically millivolts, so it reads “0 V, quite steadily” ✓.

Card 7 · Choosing the pull-up value (the centrepiece)


PlotEquationpoe, numericThe PLAN's Plot: the bus voltage as the pull-up charges the bus capacitance, with R and C sliders, the 30 % and 70 % lines, and the 300 ns (Fast) and 1000 ns (Standard) limits. Beside it is the sink current, (3.3 − 0.4) V / R, against Fast mode's 3 mA. The presets are the PLAN's Drive plus the numeric's answer, 1.77 kΩ. Switch the time axis to see F4: measured from the release, the 1.77 kΩ edge reaches 70 % at 426 ns, not on the 300 ns marker. The I²C limit is the time from 30 % to 70 %.





Presets (C = 200 pF)Time axis




Checked: 30 %→70 % = R·C·ln(0.7/0.3) = 0.8473·R·C. At 200 pF: 10 kΩ 1695 ns (0.29 mA), 4.7 kΩ 796.5 ns (0.62 mA), 1.5 kΩ 254.2 ns (1.93 mA), 820 Ω 139.0 ns (3.54 mA). Rmax = 300 ns/(0.8473 × 200 pF) = 1.770 kΩ; Rmin = 2.9 V/3 mA = 0.967 kΩ. These are the rules UM10204 gives for I²C (tr 1000/300 ns, VOL 0.4 V at 3 mA, Cb ≤ 400 pF).
