Day 1 walks the first half of the path, with a quantity that stands still. It starts at the physical
quantity and ends inside the converter, with a code. Keep the path diagram from the cold open on the
wall all day, and point at the stage you are on whenever a module begins.

**Block 1 — the quantity and the sensor (12:00–13:10).** The cold open (010) shows the whole path once,
through the eye: sense organ, pathway, processor. The measurand module (020) asks what we want to
measure and what "right" would mean, and builds the uncertainty budget the DC lab must land inside on
Day 2. Sensing (030) gives our system its sense organ: the quantity becomes electrical.

*Break 13:10–13:25.*

**Block 2 — the signal on its way in (13:25–15:30).** The sensor as a source (040) is the load-bearing
idea of the course: connect anything and you change what you measure. Current sensing (050) makes that
unavoidable — you must put something in the path. Conditioning (060) turns the shunt's millivolts into
something a converter can use, and shows that offset is amplified with the signal.

*Break 15:30–15:45.*

**Block 3 — the converter (15:45–17:30).** The ADC input (070) is the centrepiece: a switch charging a
capacitor that may not get there in time. Conversion (080) turns the held voltage into a code, and asks
how many of its bits are true. The reference (090) asks what the code is a ratio to — and closes the day
by reaching back to the sensor's excitation, where the morning began.

Every module ends with a transition that hands the signal to the next stage. Day 1 ends with a code in a
register. Day 2 opens by taking it out.
