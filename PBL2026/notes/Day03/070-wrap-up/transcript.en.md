## Where we are on the path

The whole path, walked backwards. A wrong number arrives, and the room traces it back through every stage the course has taught until they find where it went wrong.

## The arc

1. **Staged faults, presented cold** — Four or five broken measurements, across the tools we have used.
2. **The diagnostic checklist** — One line per module across three days, each a question you can ask of any measurement, in the order the signal travels the path.
3. **Questions, including the parked list** — The questions parked over three days come back now, with whatever the room still wants to ask.
4. **A number that appears is not a number you have earned** — The code runs and the number appears, but almost none of the places it can go wrong announce themselves.

## Learner actions

- Staged faults, presented cold: poe (before the statement, once per fault) — "Here is the setup and here is the reading. Where on the path
- The diagnostic checklist: checkpoint (after the statement) — "Go back to the faults we just diagnosed. Which line would have caught
- Questions, including the parked list: poll (after the statement) — "Here is everything we parked. Which do you want answered first?"
- Questions, including the parked list: qa — the floor opens once the parked list is done.
- A number that appears is not a number you have earned: checkpoint (after the statement) — "Your MCU reads 2047. What is that? Write down the first three

## Hand-off

[[transition]] That is the path, walked twice: once with a quantity that stood still, and once with one
that moved. Take the checklist with you. The next time a number appears on your bench, before you
believe it, ask it where it came from.
