Owner, 2026-10-08, accepting the 2D eye animation and closing the session: *"we can stop it now and go to the
next in a new session. you will first have to brief me on what we have to do, then i will give you the design
direction then we proceed"*.

**Why:** in that session "proceed" sent me straight into a whole unit of D1·040 specs, with design calls made
(SOURCE & LOADING drawn as a band, a Thévenin layout, an Equation family) that the owner had not seen. They also
did not know which work was in flight. A brief first keeps them in charge of direction and of the order of work.

**How to apply:** when a session opens a unit (or the owner says "proceed"), first read the tracking and the
unit's refs, then send a short brief: what the unit covers, card by card, which bench each card needs, what is
deferred and why, the visuals or design choices it will involve, and anything open from earlier units. Then stop
and wait for the owner's design direction. Only then write specs, previews or code. This extends
[[design-direction-before-building]] from single visuals to whole units. Related: [[say-safe-to-clear]],
[[authoring-first-specs-per-activity]].
