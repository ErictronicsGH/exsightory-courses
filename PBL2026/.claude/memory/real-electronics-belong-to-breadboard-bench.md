Owner, 2026-10-09, while the bench supply was being made a standalone library: *"when we do electronics with actual
images of components like what we are doing now with breadboard and power supplies and meters, that falls under the
BreadBoard Bench. so all those kind of real electronics components and devices should be put there"*, then *"they
should be modeled with the actual electronics properties for now in the animation bench lets not concern about that
and just focus on them as animation tools"*.

**Why:** a realistic part or instrument drawn as it really looks belongs to the bench that handles real electronics
(the BreadBoard Bench). Until it exists, the Animation Bench carries them.

**How to apply:**
- Model every real part or instrument with its true electrical behaviour (Ohm's law, CV/CC crossover, ratings, real
  ranges and resolution), never a look-alike that only shows pictures.
- Build them now as Animation Bench libraries (animation tools). Don't spend effort on BreadBoard Bench specs,
  contracts or migration unless the owner asks. **Superseded in part, 2026-10-11:** the owner now has the Breadboard
  Bench itself designed (U039: the room and bench, cameras, controls; no components yet), reviewed in
  bench-work/breadboard-bench/spec-review.md; components still wait for the owner's word.
- Name the BreadBoard Bench as these libraries' eventual home in their README, without doing anything about it yet.
