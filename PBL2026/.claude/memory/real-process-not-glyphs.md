Owner, 2026-10-10, on card 7's 3a parallax zoom (almond "eye" glyphs floating over a side view): *"the 3a eyes is far from real
and the process is also far from real. that is not acceptable"*.

**Why:** a glyph eye fails the realism gate ([[first-class-requirements]]) and, worse, the side view it sat in was not a real
viewpoint: parallax happens as the observer moves the head sideways in front of the dial, so a profile eye is geometrically wrong.

**How to apply:** before drawing an observer or a mechanism, work out the real viewpoint (which plane the motion happens in) and
draw it there with real parts: here, *what the eye sees* (the scale close up, the knife edge and its reflection in the mirror
strip) beside *the meter seen from above* (dial face, the pointer 1.5 mm off it, the glass, the head from above turned to the
pointer, parallel rays because the eye is far; "not to scale" said). Show the result in the instrument itself (the big meter's
reflection in its band). Related: [[animation-follows-the-flow]], [[drawing-and-numbers-agree]].
