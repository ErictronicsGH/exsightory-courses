Owner, 2026-10-08, on D1·020 card 8's meter: *"we do not really need to put in a specific product as that
will also count as given them free advertisement. although we intend to achieve 95% reality this means we
need to make things look like real but not necessarily put things like RS PRO 296568 in it. we can also
design our own realistic looking meter design and it counts as realistic"*.

**Why:** a named product on a teaching surface is free advertising, and buying or licensing a product photo
is cost for no teaching gain. Reality is in the look, the values and the behaviour, not the badge.

**How to apply:**
- **Nothing the room or a learner sees names a brand, product or part number**: no logos, model numbers or
  vendor names on a wall, a figure, a drawing, a bench label or a Flow line. Use the class instead (*a
  3½-digit handheld meter*, *a 12-bit MCU ADC input*, *a 1 % metal-film resistor*).
- **Design our own realistic instruments and parts** (a meter face, a board, a package) to the real class's
  proportions, display, ranges and specification; they count as real for the quality bar.
- **Specs may still cite a real product or datasheet as the source of a number** (A1, A8), in the spec only,
  to prove the values are real. A sourced value goes on the bench as the class's value, unbranded.
- No buying products to photograph and no licensing vendor images for this purpose.
  Related: [[bench-quality-bar]], [[render-visual-milestones]].

**Exception (owner, 2026-10-10):** the Arduino UNO photo the owner supplied for D1·030 card 1 keeps its maker's marks (ARDUINO,
UNO, the chip's mark): asked, the owner chose "keep them as photographed". Ask again for any other branded base image.

**Exception (owner, 2026-10-21, U020 D1):** D2·020 keeps its four named parts (ATmega328P, classic ESP32, ADS1115, INA226) in
the title, Flow and figures, because reading those datasheets is the module's skill. Its figures are our own redrawn extracts
citing the document numbers, with no logos or vendor page images (copyright). D1·080's ADS1115 mention stays too. Any other
module still asks.
