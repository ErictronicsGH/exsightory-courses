Owner, 2026-10-08, on the LDR-chain stills: *"the signal from each domain/interface should mimic a real
visualization of how it happens in the electronics system"* and *"make sure the flow is good enough to follow the
flow/teleprompter side so it feels natural"*.

**Why:** the presenter reads the Flow; if the wall runs ahead, lags or shows things the words never mention, the
lesson feels staged. And a symbolic glow where a real mechanism exists (photons, carriers, a field, a binary search,
a clocked bus) teaches less than the real thing.

**How to apply:**
- Map every stop to a Flow paragraph or sentence; show that mapping on the contact sheet (each frame with its
  words). Where the visual needs time the words don't give, say so and let the owner decide (never add Flow text).
- Each interface shows its real physics at its real values: e.g. photons as wave packets, photoconduction, current
  and potential in a divider, a field with ≈ no current on a signal core, RC removing fast noise but not hum, SAR
  trials, firmware arithmetic, I²C clock/data. Put the mechanism in a zoom panel above the part (part of the demo,
  not a reveal card) and keep in-place motion on the part.
- Label illustrative shapes as illustrative; state slowed time honestly.
