Never trim, shorten, condense or remove the user's content (Flow prose, card statements, notes,
drafted material) — not to fit a time budget, not for tidiness. Report an overrun (e.g. module
words vs targetMinutes) and let the user decide what to cut. This applies to subagents too:
brief them explicitly.

**Why:** the user said "do not do any trimming by yourself… so you don't mistakenly trim something
important to me" (2026-10-07).
**How to apply:** budgets guide the length of NEW drafted content only; existing text is untouchable
unless the user asks. Related: [[feedback-natural-order]], [[pbl2026-course-structure]].
