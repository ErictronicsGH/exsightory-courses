Owner, 2026-10-08: bench activities are *"intended for quality visualization and learning purposes
so it must always be implemented the best way that will foster interactivity, intuition, insight and
understanding and also they should look nicer, and great. The target is to capture above 95 %
reality in the visualization so that our virtual benches are not much different from the real
world."* The example given was the eye section as a very good 3D rendering with proper
interactivity and a stepped flow.

**Why:** the benches stand in for real instruments and real phenomena. If they look or behave
unlike the real thing, the intuition built on them is wrong.

**How to apply:** every spec in `bench-work/specs/` passes `.claude/standards/bench-quality-bar.md`
(real values and units, real behaviour including the non-ideal, a faithfulness checklist, designed
interaction, finished looks). Never settle for a diagram of it where the real thing can be shown.
Repeated 2026-10-08 for every animation and visualization (*"alway note we want to achieve reality as much as
possible in our animations and visualizations"*): draw the real object (shaded glass, enamelled copper, brass posts,
an instrument's bezel and display), not an icon of it, keeping the course's dark ground and neon accents.
