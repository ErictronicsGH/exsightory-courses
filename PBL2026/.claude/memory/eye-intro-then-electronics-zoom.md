Owner, 2026-10-08, on the first running build (which reused the eye animation's head-and-brain view for p1–p3):
*"why is a part of the eye animation in the electronics? i only need a still image just as an intro"* and
*"the opening scene should have a still image of the full eye animation captured the the electronics half beneath and
visually mapping the various sections of the eye diagram that corresponds to the electronics part. but the moment the
electronics animation begins to play the eye section should hide and give the full stage to the electronics full view
then as the next steps focus on particular zones those focused zones zooms into focus. when all the steps are done the
full view of the electronics with complete signals flowing should be displayed"*. Stop 8 (p6–p7): electronics only.

**Why:** the eye was taught in its own animation; here it is only the bridge into the electronics, so it must not
compete with or re-teach it.

**How to apply** (this card, and the pattern for later "analogy → system" animations):
- Opening: the eye's full process as one still (the accepted animation captured), the system beneath, the matching
  sections visibly mapped (colour, brackets, connectors). No eye close-ups or eye motion.
- Play: the still hides; the system gets the whole stage at full view.
- Each step: the camera zooms into that step's zone (part + its zoom panel), then on.
- After the last step: zoom out to the full view with every signal flowing end to end.
