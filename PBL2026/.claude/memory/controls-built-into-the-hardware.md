Owner, 2026-10-08, on the LDR Explore build (sliders first sat in a panel, then in a card beside the lamp):
*"the controls could be nicely attached to the light itself … it should have the effect like the light has the controls
built in remember realism is important"*. Accepted result: an inline rocker switch and thumbwheel dimmer on the lamp's
cord, a tuner head (display, band selector, tuning knob) under it, a film selector and a divider swap switch on the
sensor board. The owner: *"i love what i am seeing"*.

**Why:** a control that looks like the real object's own control teaches how the real thing is operated and keeps the
scene one believable apparatus; floating UI breaks the realism the quality bar asks for.

**How to apply:** put each control where the real device would carry it, drawn in the scene's own style and moving
with the camera; the readouts that are measurements (graphs, spectra) may stay in a side panel. Where the control is
idealised (no real lamp spans radio to gamma), say so on the object. Light follows real optics too: a lamp lights only
within its shade's cone (the shade swivels to aim). Related: [[bench-quality-bar]], [[animation-player-controls]],
[[design-direction-before-building]].
