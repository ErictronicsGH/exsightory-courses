# Instruments, drawn as you watch

Three drawings built up in steps, and one that is simply a picture.

## What sits inside a moving-coil meter

```drawing
title: Inside a moving-coil meter
src: shared/drawings/moving-coil-meter.benchdrawing
alt: A moving-coil meter seen end on. Two magnet poles, north on the left and south on the right, face a round soft-iron core with a rectangular coil wound on it, a flat spiral hairspring winds out from the pivot at the centre, and a pointer rises from the centre to the upper right towards the full-scale mark.
caption: The pole faces are shaped to a circle, so the field across the coil is the same wherever it turns.
```

## A Wheatstone bridge as its parts sit

```drawing
title: A Wheatstone bridge
src: shared/drawings/wheatstone-bridge.benchdrawing
alt: Four resistors on the sides of a diamond, labelled R1, R2, R3 and Rx, with a supply across the top and bottom corners and a galvanometer marked G across the left and right corners.
caption: At balance the galvanometer reads zero, and Rx = R1 × R3 / R2.
```

The bridge is drawn as a picture of where the parts sit, rather than as a
schematic.

## A strain gauge stretching with its beam

```drawing
title: A strain gauge on a loaded beam
src: shared/drawings/strain-gauge-beam.benchdrawing
alt: A horizontal beam fixed to a wall at its left end, a zig-zag strain gauge bonded to its top face, and a downward force arrow at its free right end, with a dashed line showing the beam bending under the load.
caption: The top face stretches under the load, and the gauge's grid stretches with it.
```

## Where a thermocouple's two wires meet

```drawing
title: A thermocouple's hot junction
src: shared/drawings/thermocouple-junction.benchdrawing
alt: Two wires, chromel above and alumel below, running from the left and meeting at a single welded point on the right labelled hot junction.
```

A drawing with no steps is a picture: it shows whole, and there is nothing for
