# Drawn in another editor

Three drawings made in LibreOffice Draw, exported as SVG and imported with
`insight-draw import`. Their names, layers and steps were added by hand
afterwards. The sources are in `bench-courses/draw/svg/`.

```drawing
title: A moving-coil meter in section
src: shared/drawings/meter-section.benchdrawing
alt: A moving-coil meter cut through. A permanent magnet's north pole on the left and south pole on the right stand on a yoke, their faces curved around a round soft-iron core. The coil's sides sit in the gap, marked with crosses on the left where current goes in and dots on the right where it comes out. A pointer rises from a pivot at the centre to a curved scale above.
caption: The pole faces and the core keep the gap even, so the field the coil turns in is the same at every angle.
```

```drawing
title: A strain gauge on a loaded cantilever
src: shared/drawings/gauge-on-beam.benchdrawing
alt: A beam fixed in a hatched wall on the left, a strain gauge with a zig-zag grid bonded to its top face near the wall, its two leads running up to the bridge, and a downward load F at the free end. Arrows above the gauge pull outwards for tension, and arrows below the beam push inwards for compression.
caption: The gauge sits where the bending is greatest, and stretches with the face it is bonded to.
```

```drawing
title: A sine wave drawn from its phasor
src: shared/drawings/waveform-construction.benchdrawing
alt: On the left, a phasor turning in a circle, with spokes every thirty degrees. Horizontal lines carry each spoke's height across to a time axis on the right, and a sine wave is drawn through the points, labelled v equals Vm sine omega t.
caption: The wave is the height of the turning phasor, plotted against the angle it has turned.
```
