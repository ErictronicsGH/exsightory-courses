# Resistance in practice

A reading of resistance in practice is only as good as the way it was taken. Note the range, the connection and the moment, then write the number down before you interpret it (0).

## Take it yourself

```checkpoint
instructions: |
  Take one reading of resistance in practice on the bench in front of you.
items:
  - Set the range before you connect
  - Write the reading down with its unit
```
