Start with the story, not the circuit: everyone in the room has shorted
something. [[chat]] ask who has, and let the hands go up before saying anything
about the schematic.

Then the sense resistor. Draw it before showing the finished loop — the whole
idea is that a small voltage across a small resistor is a current measurement,
and that lands better if they see the resistor go in first.

[[demo]] short the output on the bench supply and let them watch the meter hold.
This is the moment the feature stops being abstract.

[[pause]] before foldback. It is the part people conflate with plain limiting,
and it needs a clean break from what came before.

Close on the dissipation number. The transistor is hottest into a dead short,
which is exactly when everyone assumes it is safe because "nothing is running".
