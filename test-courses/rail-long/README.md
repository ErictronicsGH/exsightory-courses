# The long-part rail course

An invented course with **one long part between two short ones**, for Study's contents rail (crossing 1022,
Studio's M64g-iii and M64g-iv): the rail windows a long part's sections round the reader's. The middle part has seven
sections and a title long enough to take two lines; its third section, `02-readings-resistance-out-of-circuit`, is
nine slides by the presenter's grouping (`groupIntoCards`). Every other section is three or four. Each section
closes on a checkpoint, so the audit has nothing to say.

| Part | Sections | Section ids |
|---|---|---|
| `01-setup` | 2 | `01-setup-the-meter`, `01-setup-the-leads` |
| `02-readings` | 7 | `02-readings-voltage-across-a-part`, `02-readings-current-through-a-branch`, `02-readings-resistance-out-of-circuit`, `02-readings-continuity`, `02-readings-diode-test`, `02-readings-capacitance`, `02-readings-frequency` |
| `03-records` | 3 | `03-records-the-lab-book`, `03-records-uncertainty`, `03-records-the-report` |

Built with Core's compiler, as `../test-course/` is (its README, *Building it*). Zero warnings is correct.
