# The meter

A reading of the meter is only as good as the way it was taken. Note the range, the connection and the moment, then write the number down before you interpret it (0).

## Take it yourself

```checkpoint
instructions: |
  Take one reading of the meter on the bench in front of you.
items:
  - Set the range before you connect
  - Write the reading down with its unit
```
